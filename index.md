### About the App

Hearthside — Large-Print Puzzles offers five kinds of puzzles with many levels each:

- **Word Cipher** — crack a substitution code to reveal a charming country saying.
- **Dot-to-Dot** — tap the numbered dots in order to reveal a hidden picture.
- **Picture Logic** — shade squares using the number clues to uncover a small picture.
- **Winding Maze** — trace a path with your finger from the start to the finish.
- **Word Search** — find hidden garden words by dragging across the grid.

Your progress is saved automatically on your device.

### How to Play

**Word Cipher** — Every letter has been swapped for another. Tap a coded letter, then tap the letter you think it really stands for — every matching letter fills in at once. Tap **Give me a hint** if you get stuck.

**Dot-to-Dot** — Tap the dots in order, starting at **1**. A line is drawn each time you find the next number. Connect them all to reveal the picture.

**Picture Logic** — The numbers on each row and column tell you how many squares in a run are shaded. Tap a square once to shade it, again to mark it with an X, and again to clear it. Fill the correct squares to reveal the picture.

**Winding Maze** — Press on the green dot and drag your finger through the maze to the checkered flag. Slide back the way you came to undo a wrong turn. Tap **Start over** to reset.

**Word Search** — Press the first letter of a word and drag to the last letter. Words run across, down, or diagonally. Found words are crossed off the list below the grid.

### Frequently Asked Questions

**Does the app collect my data?**
No. Hearthside does not collect, store, or share any personal data. See the [Privacy Policy](#privacy-policy) below for details.

**Do I need an internet connection?**
No. All puzzles work fully offline. An internet connection is only used if you choose to tap the link to the related book on Amazon.

**How is my progress saved?**
Completed puzzles are saved locally on your device. Deleting the app will erase this progress.

**The print is still hard to read — can I make it bigger?**
The app is already designed for large print. You can also increase system text size in **Settings → Accessibility → Display & Text Size** on your device.

**Is there a paperback version?**
Yes. This app is a free companion to the *Hearthside Large-Print Variety Puzzles* paperback series, which contains many more puzzles in each theme. You can find it on Amazon via the link on the home screen.

**I found a bug or have a suggestion.**
We'd love to hear from you — please email the address above with a short description (and your device model and iOS version, if it's a bug).

## Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** July 7, 2026

### Summary

Hearthside — Large-Print Puzzles ("the App") **does not collect, store, or share any personal data.** There are no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

### Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of the following:

- Personal information (name, email address, phone number, etc.)
- Location data
- Contacts, photos, microphone, or camera
- Usage analytics or behavioral tracking data
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA) or any device identifiers
- Crash logs or diagnostic data sent to us

The App has no user accounts and requires no registration to play.

### Information Stored on Your Device

To remember which puzzles you have completed, the App saves your puzzle progress **locally on your device only**, using Apple's standard on-device storage (UserDefaults). This information:

- Never leaves your device
- Is not transmitted to us or to any third party
- Contains only which puzzles have been marked as solved
- Is removed when you delete the App

We have no ability to access this information.

### Network Use and External Links

The App itself works fully offline and makes no network requests to us.

The App contains a link to a related paperback book on Amazon.com. If you tap that link, your device will open Amazon in your web browser or the Amazon app. At that point you leave the App, and any information you provide is handled by Amazon under **Amazon's own privacy policy**, not this one. We do not receive any data about your visit to or purchases on Amazon.

### Children's Privacy

The App is a general-audience puzzle app intended for adults and seniors. It does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Because the App collects no data at all, it does not gather personal information from children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country).

### Third-Party Services

The App does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

### Changes to This Policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this location with a new effective date. Because the App collects no data, we do not expect material changes.
